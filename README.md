# Instoryview App

**Instoryview for Android and iOS: track Instagram follower and following changes from your phone.** The app is a client of the same account and data as [instoryview.com](https://instoryview.com).

## Features

- Follower and following tracking for the public accounts you add, with the changes Instoryview has detected.
- A feed of detected changes across your tracked accounts, and a per-account timeline.
- Change reports and, optionally, a daily summary notification at an hour you choose. It is sent only when something was detected.
- Exports of the available follower and following lists.
- A free story viewer and profile lookup for public accounts.
- Credits, plans and the affiliate program, managed in the same account.

## Notes

- Changes are reported as *detected* between scans; Instagram does not provide follow timestamps.
- No Instagram password is requested. Sign in with Google or an email link.
- Account owners can remove their account from Instoryview at [instoryview.com/removal](https://instoryview.com/removal).
